Smak Stats — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · Applies to the Smak Stats Android app by Blazecraft Studio.

English

What data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, Smak Stats requests read-only access to your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) and to YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly). It reads channel information, video list and statistics, comments on your videos, and analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers, traffic sources, countries, age and gender).

How the data is used

The data is used only to display statistics, charts, a home-screen widget and notifications to you inside the app. It is stored only on your phone (in the app’s private storage) and is deleted when you sign out or uninstall the app.

No sharing, no servers

The app talks directly to Google’s APIs. We do not operate any server, do not receive your data, and do not share, sell or transfer it to anyone. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. The app never modifies anything on your channel.

Google API Services

Smak Stats’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can sign out in the app at any time, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page. Contact: com.blazecraft.app@gmail.com
