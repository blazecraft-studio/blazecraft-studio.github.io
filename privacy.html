Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Applies to all watch faces and apps published by Blazecraft Studio on Google Play, including Blaze Digital, Hunter System, Crime Lab and Neon Grid (Lite and Pro versions).

English

Summary

Our watch faces do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

Health and activity data

Our watch faces display your heart rate and step count. These values are provided by the Wear OS system on your watch and are only shown on the watch screen. They never leave your device, are not saved by us and are not sent to us or to any third party. The watch face requests the “Body sensors” and “Physical activity” permissions only to display these values; you can revoke them at any time in the watch settings.

No internet access, no tracking

The watch face has no internet permission, contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not use cookies.

Purchases

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google’s privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

Our products are suitable for all ages and do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page. Contact: com.blazecraft.app@gmail.com
