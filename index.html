Install in a minute

Works on Wear OS 4 and newer: Samsung Galaxy Watch 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, Ultra, Google Pixel Watch and others.

Open Google Play

On your phone or watch, find “Blaze Digital” and choose your watch as the install device.

Set as watch face

Touch and hold the current watch face, swipe to Blaze Digital and tap it.

Make it yours

Touch and hold → Customize: pick colors, fonts, styles, background and shortcuts.
