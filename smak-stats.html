Smak Stats

YouTube channel statistics for the cooking channel Smak-Story · Android app by Blazecraft Studio

English

Smak Stats is a personal Android app that shows statistics of the Smak-Story YouTube channel to its owners: subscribers over time, views, watch time, audience (traffic sources, countries, age and gender), top videos and recent comments, plus a home-screen widget and notifications about new comments and subscribers.

The app signs in with Google and requests read-only access to YouTube (youtube.readonly) and YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly). It never changes anything on the channel.
